What We Treat

Comprehensive Eye Care Services

From your first eye exam to advanced surgery, we provide the full spectrum of ophthalmologic care under one roof.

🔬

Routine Eye Exams

Comprehensive vision screening and eye health assessments for all ages. Early detection saves vision.

🏥

Cataract Surgery

Advanced cataract removal with premium intraocular lens implants for clearer vision post-surgery.

✨

LASIK & Refractive Surgery

Laser vision correction to reduce or eliminate dependence on glasses and contact lenses.

🫀

Glaucoma Treatment

Diagnosis and ongoing management of glaucoma to prevent irreversible vision loss.

👶

Pediatric Ophthalmology

Specialized eye care for children including amblyopia, strabismus, and childhood vision disorders.

🧬

Retina & Vitreous Care

Diagnosis and treatment of retinal conditions including detachment, macular degeneration, and diabetic retinopathy.
