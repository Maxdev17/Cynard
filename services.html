What We Offer

Our Eye Care Services

From routine vision checks to complex surgical procedures — we provide the full spectrum of ophthalmologic care under one roof in Port Harcourt.

Core Services

Treatments & Procedures

Every service delivered by certified ophthalmologists using advanced diagnostic equipment.

🔬

Routine Eye Examinations

Comprehensive vision screenings and eye health assessments for all ages. We check visual acuity, eye pressure, peripheral vision, and overall ocular health. Early detection of problems like glaucoma and diabetic eye disease starts here.

🏥

Cataract Surgery

Advanced cataract removal using modern phacoemulsification technique with premium intraocular lens (IOL) implants. We restore clarity to vision clouded by cataracts with minimal recovery time and outstanding outcomes.

✨

LASIK & Refractive Surgery

Laser vision correction to permanently reduce or eliminate dependence on glasses and contact lenses. Our team evaluates your candidacy carefully and uses precision laser technology to reshape your cornea for sharper vision.

🫀

Glaucoma Diagnosis & Treatment

Glaucoma is the leading cause of irreversible blindness — but it is manageable when caught early. We offer comprehensive glaucoma screening, IOP monitoring, medical management, and surgical intervention where needed.

🧬

Retina & Vitreous Care

Specialized care for retinal conditions including retinal detachment, macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and vitreous disorders. Our retina specialists use advanced imaging and laser technology to protect and restore your central vision.

👶

Pediatric Ophthalmology

Dedicated eye care for infants, children, and teenagers. We diagnose and treat amblyopia (lazy eye), strabismus (squint), childhood myopia, congenital conditions, and learning-related vision problems in a child-friendly environment.

💎

Oculoplastics

Surgical and non-surgical procedures involving the eyelids, orbit, and tear duct system. This includes droopy eyelid correction (ptosis repair), eyelid lesion removal, and treatment of blocked tear ducts.

👓

Contact Lens & Optical Services

Professional contact lens fitting and prescription optical services. Our optometrists assess your vision needs and fit you with the most appropriate lens type — from daily disposables to specialty lenses for irregular corneas.
